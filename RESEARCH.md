# Options desk research

The desk compares expected net P&L at a specified order size and time horizon. The same decision must survive plausible deterioration in fills and exits, a per-order loss budget, and an independent portfolio stress test. Capital stays in cash when the evidence is incomplete or the price is too high.

## Research priorities

1. **Start with execution.** An option’s quoted spread, effective spread, and exit costs are different measurements. Record every proposed limit, the quote and depth at submission, the order lifetime, the captured fill or explicit no-fill, and subsequent markouts. Group fills by side, size, time to expiry, and market regime. Score expected P&L per attempted order and tail loss conditional on a fill. Comparing the same underlying forecast across entry prices requires a separate fill-and-exit distribution at each tested price. [Timing and effective spreads](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa010) · [Complex-order execution](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/options/trading/complex-orders)
2. **Test event direction and event volatility independently.** Separate the known earnings jump from ordinary day-to-day volatility. Fit the market-implied distribution from synchronized option quotes, with diagnostics on strike and maturity consistency. Learn the real-world jump distribution separately, with partial pooling across issuers to limit small-sample error. Published earnings studies report both positive returns for specific straddle windows and expensive event protection when implied-volatility curves are concave. Test the windows and quote rules individually. [Scheduled-jump valuation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.8414) · [Earnings straddles](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/abs/anticipating-uncertainty-straddles-around-earnings-announcements/7B34877AD5E06304BA3C55FBA3219FDD) · [Implied-volatility curvature and event risk](https://academic.oup.com/rof/article/29/4/963/8079062)
3. **Treat gamma as measurable sensitivity.** Compute the book’s own gamma from positions and reprice under joint spot, skew, liquidity, and time shocks. For dealer hedging, compare scenarios with different plausible inventory signs when the desk only has public open interest. Test incremental predictive value only where a credible signed-position feed is available. Cboe’s SPX analysis found balanced 0DTE flow in its sample. Gross volume alone cannot determine hedge direction or magnitude. [Cboe’s position-based analysis](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/0-dt-es-decoded-positioning-trends-and-market-impact)
4. **Make advanced models earn their place.** Challenge a realized-volatility baseline with fractional or rough-volatility forecasts and a regularized baseline with a pooled tree model. Score probability calibration and execution-adjusted returns separately on chronological holdouts. Refit only on information available by each decision time. Include all variants in the trial ledger, keep contemporaneous events together, and check whether performance survives worse fills and removing the largest winner. Published nonlinear option portfolio results motivate a challenger under our own horizon and trading costs. [Rough volatility](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3394) · [Option return prediction](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/36/9/3548/7056660) · [Backtest overfitting](https://sdm.lbl.gov/oapapers/ssrn-id2507040-bailey.pdf)

## First forward comparison

Use the registered earnings lab’s post-release cutoff at 9:45 a.m. ET and exit at 3:15 p.m. ET. Compare a price-only rule with a challenger that adds the change from the issuer’s prior published guidance range to its new range. Both use the same return horizon, QQQ benchmark, cutoffs, and quote policy. The [frozen research plan](data/plans/post-earnings-guidance-challenger.json) specifies the threshold, enrollment, and treatment of withdrawn or incomparable guidance. Preserve the source excerpt, publication timestamp, and receipt timestamp. An unavailable guidance feature produces a coverage gap and the stock outcome is still scored. Score option P&L only where the evidence supports the intended trade and size.

Begin with Salesforce and include Micron as a distinct issuer event. These observations can exercise the workflow. Model promotion requires a larger unseen cohort with matched benchmark coverage and positive incremental expectancy after costs. The existing tape and earnings replays remain development evidence.

## Limit-price evaluator

Run `python3 optionsdesk.py price-limits examples/price-limits.json` to reproduce the synthetic example. Prices are points on a standard 100-share contract. Model exit proceeds and fees are dollars per filled contract. Each price has a baseline distribution and an adverse-selection distribution with full, partial, or zero fills. Return distributions and their availability times are supplied inputs. Fractional probability mass at the tail cutoff is handled explicitly. The result reports the highest tested price that passed, the tested price with the best worst-model expectancy, and the reasons for rejecting each other price. Passing prices can be disjoint. See the example for the input schema. [Expected shortfall for discrete loss distributions](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378426602002716)

## Volatility and nonlinear risk

The desk uses Black–Scholes–Merton as the reference for Greeks and exact stress repricing. Exchange market prices remain the source of entry and exit marks. The advanced risk report attributes joint moves in spot, IV and time by averaging their marginal effect over all six application orders. Strike-specific IV shifts expose skew risk. The event variance diagnostic estimates how much total variance the surface associates with an announcement under a constant background-variance assumption. It refuses mismatched or asynchronous observations and retains quote uncertainty. A later surface model can compete on leave-one-strike-out error against the observed bid/ask bands and must satisfy butterfly and calendar constraints before it can replace the current diagnostics. [Black–Scholes](https://doi.org/10.1086/260062) · [Arbitrage-free SVI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.0646)

The [first volatility benchmark](data/volatility-power-law-challenger.json) compares a fixed power-law weighting of squared returns with EWMA and a 20-session mean on nine symbols and 37 non-overlapping five-session windows. The power-law filter slightly improves mean QLIKE and leads on seven symbols. The symbols share the same dates. Historical daily closes give a noisy variance proxy, and the benchmark measures neither options pricing nor P&L. A calibrated rough-volatility model remains a separate challenger. [Volatility proxy evaluation](https://public.econ.duke.edu/~ap172/Patton_vol_proxies_JoE_2011.pdf) · [Rough volatility](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3394)

Compare learned hedging policies with three fixed baselines on identical paths. Use an unhedged position, delta rebalancing, and a no-trade band with width fixed before the evaluation. Compare net results, turnover, and tail loss across separate held-out sessions with wider spreads, larger latency, and gap moves. Keep unpriced liquidations visible when assessing coverage and tail risk. The bundled replay exercises fill mechanics on a synthetic path. A learned policy earns further development by improving held-out outcomes under those same constraints. [Hedging bands under transaction costs](https://doi.org/10.1017/S095679259900368X) · [Bank of Japan IMES review of deep hedging and calibration](https://www.imes.boj.or.jp/research/abstracts/english/24-E-02.html)

## Research selected from Argus

- Use a chronological holdout for promotion evidence. Purge overlapping outcome windows during model selection and log every candidate, including discarded variants. Deflated Sharpe depends on the effective number of independent trials and the distribution of trial Sharpes. Raw worktree count and a threshold by themselves cannot establish edge. Freeze cost-stress assumptions before the holdout and report net expectancy separately from portfolio Expected Shortfall and loss under specified crash scenarios. [Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf)
- Treat expiration controls as contract- and session-specific. Robinhood can intervene in the final 30 minutes and may act outside that window. Before relying on any live workflow, verify available order types, broker and venue handling of multi-leg packages, and actual reconciliation coverage. Compare complete timestamped positions, cash and working orders before considering account state matched. Start with a read-only reconciliation report. [Robinhood expiration policy](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/expiration-exercise-and-assignment/) · [Cboe complex orders](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/options/trading/complex-orders)

## Prospective validation priorities from Neon

- Start with a narrow, predeclared cohort and capture both market time and receipt time on the exact contracts. The current timed worker covers registered cutoffs. Subsecond and five-second markouts need timestamped captures around the fill, and measured paper slippage needs actual attempt records. Confirm OPRA access explicitly. Indicative quotes can support development but cannot qualify as OPRA execution evidence. [Alpaca real-time option feeds](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-option-data)
- For paired outcomes, retain every predeclared opportunity. A deliberate cash decision or confirmed no-fill can have zero trading P&L. An unresolved exit has unknown P&L. Report joint coverage and run a missing-data sensitivity analysis before treating observed pairs as representative. Group inference by entry date and account for overlap across holding windows.
- Test the profitability hypothesis separately from an expression change. The negative earnings closing-quote overlay cannot identify why the sample lost money. Compare cash, a fixed liquid structure, and a challenger on common opportunities, with the same evidence cutoffs and cost stress. If event text is a challenger, freeze the extraction schema and numerical baseline before receiving the live releases and score incremental calibration and decision value.
- The first event-text comparison uses paired return quantiles at 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90%. Confirm the exact issuer release before scoring and look at tail loss and coverage separately from average loss. Measure the text arm on the same post-release prices as the versioned numerical arm. If the cited features improve the forecast, the next comparison should add a cheaper text baseline before attributing value to the extractor. [Quantile scoring rules](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437)

- Use option-implied probabilities as a pricing diagnostic. Any KL divergence between physical and risk-neutral distributions is dimensionless. A candidate's costs and expected payout are in dollars and depend on its exact payoff and execution terms. Compare the expected net payout of each tradable shape under the physical forecast and stress its losses separately. A joint distribution is needed to optimize log growth of the entire book. [Option-implied risk-neutral distributions](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr677.html)
- Evaluate the overnight and intraday variance components separately before testing a variance carry strategy. Compare a simple recent-jump count or EWMA risk filter with any fitted Hawkes process on the same timestamped stress episodes. Use realized covariance concentration as a baseline for any entropy measure. The feature earns a place if it improves held-out outcomes after costs.

- Use markouts at 1, 5, 10, and 60 seconds and five minutes from timestamped paper fills to separate what we paid from what prices did immediately afterward. Compare outcomes for aggressive and passive orders only after there is enough coverage on both groups. Top-of-book and trades support quote diagnostics. OPRA.PILLAR cannot establish a venue queue or recover participant-level positions. [Option auction research](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/39/3/783/8193725) · [OPRA dataset specification](https://databento.com/docs/datasets/opra-pillar)
- Benchmark variance forecasts against a fixed HAR-RV model using daily, weekly, and monthly realized measures, alongside the current EWMA. Use timestamped intraday returns and handle the overnight interval explicitly. Keep forward windows and training label availability identical for every challenger. [Corsi's HAR model](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~hauser/LVs/FinEtricsQF/References/Corsi2009JFinEtrics_LMmodelRealizedVola.pdf)
- Treat event-contract prices as a candidate input to Catalyst. Start with one macro series, matching release, vintage, transformation, threshold, and settlement rule. Use the event market to forecast the macro outcome and estimate the equity response to that outcome conditional on regime and expectations. Compare the resulting option payoff forecast with the executable chain. Test incremental value over a model using options and public information alone. [Federal Reserve research on macro prediction markets](https://doi.org/10.17016/FEDS.2026.010) · [CPI contract terms](https://assets.kalshi.com/regulatory/product-certifications/CPI.pdf)
